Privacy Policy for π TEX

Last Updated: March 2024

Overview

π TEX is a browser extension designed to help users extract and copy LaTeX equations from AI chat interfaces. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

What We Do

π TEX is a simple, focused tool that:

  • Helps you copy mathematical equations in LaTeX format from supported AI chat platforms
  • Operates entirely within your browser
  • Stores minimal user preferences locally

Data Collection and Usage

What We Access

  • Website Content: We only access the content of the current page on supported platforms (ChatGPT, Deepseek, and Abacus AI) to identify and extract LaTeX equations
  • Clipboard: We use clipboard access only when you explicitly choose to copy an equation
  • Local Storage: We store your preferences locally in your browser

What We Don't Do

  • We do not collect personal information
  • We do not track your browsing history
  • We do not store or transmit your chat contents
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools
  • We do not share any data with third parties

Permissions Explained

Our extension requires minimal permissions to function:

1. clipboardWrite

  • Used only to copy LaTeX equations to your clipboard
  • Activated only when you explicitly click to copy
  • No clipboard monitoring or reading

2. storage

  • Stores your preferences locally in your browser
  • No data is transmitted externally
  • You can clear this data through your browser settings

3. Host Permissions

  • Limited to specific AI chat websites:
    • chatgpt.com
    • chat.deepseek.com
    • apps.abacus.ai
  • Required only to identify and extract LaTeX equations
  • No access to other websites

Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored locally on your device
  • No external servers or databases are used
  • No data is transmitted over the internet
  • No user accounts or login required

Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Clear local storage through your browser settings
  • Disable or remove the extension at any time
  • Control clipboard access through browser settings

Changes to This Policy

We will update this privacy policy as needed to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The date at the top of this policy indicates when it was last updated.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or our privacy practices, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.

Compliance

π TEX complies with the Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies and is committed to:

  • Not selling or transferring user data to third parties
  • Not using data for purposes unrelated to the extension's core functionality
  • Not using data for creditworthiness or lending purposes

This privacy policy is available in our GitHub repository and may be updated periodically. Please check back for any updates.
